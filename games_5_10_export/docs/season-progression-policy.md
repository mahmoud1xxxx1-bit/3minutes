# Season progression policy

- Ranked seasons are approximately 30 days and remain the only source of RP/rank movement.
- Season area exposes a prominent Missions gateway in addition to season/rank information.
- Missions include daily and weekly objectives; reward claims are server-authoritative.
- Season Pass has 30 levels at 500 Season XP per level.
- Free and Premium tracks are separate claim paths.
- Premium access is season-scoped; a new season requires a new Premium Season Pass entitlement.
- Legendary prestige history and permanent Prestige Stars survive season rollover.
- Historical rank showcase selections never alter the player's current competitive rank.
