# Premium Season Pass policy

- Product: `premium_season_pass_30d`.
- Google Play base plan: `prepaid-30d`.
- Reference price: USD 30 per 30-day season; storefronts display the localized Google Play price.
- The pass unlocks only the active season and does not auto-carry into the next season.
- A verified purchase token can unlock only one season and cannot be shared across accounts.
- Premium access is persisted only after Google Play server verification.
- Premium Prestige Stars are earned through play, not granted immediately for payment.
- One permanent Prestige Star is available at Premium levels 6, 12, 18, 24, and 30; maximum 5 Premium-pass Stars per season.
- Reward claims and Star awards are server-authoritative and idempotent.
- Premium rewards remain cosmetic/progression-oriented and provide no direct match-strength advantage.
- Production activation requires Blaze-deployed Functions plus Google Play Console product/base-plan configuration and Android Publisher API access.
