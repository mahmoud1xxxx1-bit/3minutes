# Cosmetics and ownership policy

- Cosmetics never change match rules, score formulas, difficulty, timer, evidence validation, RP gains, or matchmaking fairness.
- Every catalog item can be previewed before ownership; locked items remain visible and previewable.
- Coin purchases are granted atomically by server authority with balance validation.
- Prestige Star cosmetics are threshold unlocks; Prestige Stars are permanent status and are never consumed.
- Google Play cosmetic purchases are persisted only after server verification and support restore.
- Achievement/season-exclusive cosmetics are granted only after server-side requirement validation.
- Owned items remain durable across sign-out/sign-in and device reinstall once the authoritative entitlement exists.
- Equipped cosmetics must already be owned/earned/unlocked.
- Avatar catalog contract: exactly 45 avatars — 5 free, 20 Coins, 10 premium, 5 Prestige-Star thresholds, 5 achievement/season exclusives.
