# 3 Minutes — Device Field QA Remediation Ledger

Date: 2026-08-19
Status: IN PROGRESS
Source: physical Android device testing after validated debug APK.

This ledger records issues discovered by real-device testing after the previous CI closeout. It is intentionally separate from CI evidence: a green build does not prove visual quality, first-run polish, navigation discoverability, asset loading latency, or audio experience.

## Confirmed device findings

1. Avatar artwork quality was below the intended premium visual bar for several of the 45 avatars.
2. Avatar/shop artwork could show loading placeholders/spinners while browsing; first experience was not acceptably prewarmed.
3. Premium Season Pass was not discoverable enough from the Season surface even though its component existed inside Progression.
4. Internal infrastructure terminology was visible to players (including Spark/Blaze/server-authority wording). Internal plan/deployment names must never be player-facing.
5. Sign out occurred without a confirmation dialog.
6. Daily/weekly missions could become stuck behind an indefinite season-loading spinner when no current season document was available to the current backend.
7. No dedicated player Settings screen existed.
8. No persistent music/SFX volume controls existed.
9. No main-menu ambient music or in-match focus music was wired at the time of the device report.
10. Legacy profile editing still contained obsolete `default_01..04` avatar IDs, conflicting with the approved 45-avatar ownership catalog.

## Avatar root cause — confirmed

The previous runtime used five base64-encoded WebP atlases. `free_atlas.webp.b64` decodes to only **480×96 px**, which means each of its five portraits is effectively about **96×96 px** before being enlarged in Shop/Profile preview. This is a source-resolution defect, not a device rendering defect. The same architecture also required runtime base64 decoding and asynchronous image delivery, explaining the visible loading state reported during device testing.

### Avatar remediation implemented

Commit `68f2b9177382befa00ca580ead1349b32dcfb944` replaces the low-resolution atlas runtime in `AvatarArtwork` with deterministic local vector portraits rendered through Flutter `CustomPainter`.

Properties of the new runtime:

- Preserves all existing 45 cosmetic IDs, acquisition rules, ownership records and equipment contracts.
- No Base64 decode.
- No WebP atlas dependency at runtime.
- No `FutureBuilder` image wait.
- No avatar loading spinner/placeholder cycle while browsing.
- Resolution independent: portraits are painted from a 1024-unit logical canvas and remain sharp at preview/profile/card sizes.
- Maintains category differentiation across Free / Coins / Premium / Prestige Stars / Exclusive with stronger halos, armor, emblems and crowns in higher-value tiers.
- `AvatarArtwork.preloadAll()` remains as a compatibility no-op so the shell startup contract is not broken.

## Other remediation already implemented in source

- Main menu Settings entry exists.
- Settings stores game-music and SFX volume/mute through SharedPreferences and provides a sound preview.
- Sign out from Home/Settings requires explicit confirmation.
- Home profile hero renders the player's real approved avatar ID rather than a generic person icon.
- `SeasonHubScreen` resolves the current season with a two-second timeout and opens a safe preview state instead of leaving Missions behind an endless spinner.
- Premium Season Pass now has a direct Season entry and dedicated screen.
- Player-facing Season/Premium unavailable text uses neutral product language instead of exposing Spark/Blaze/Firebase-plan names.
- Premium Season Pass policy remains $30 per 30-day season with up to five permanent Prestige Stars earned through play at levels 6/12/18/24/30.
- Legacy `default_01..04` avatar selection path has been removed from the approved profile/economy flow.
- A generated, license-independent audio engine exists for menu ambience, match-focus music and interface/reward/start SFX; final all-mode match wiring is still part of this remediation cycle.

## Remediation policy

- No internal Firebase plan names, backend phases, deployment gates, or Cloud Functions terminology may appear in player-facing copy.
- Player-facing unavailable states must use neutral product language only.
- Missions/Progression must fail soft and must never show an endless spinner solely because no live season is returned.
- Premium Season Pass must be surfaced prominently from Season, not hidden behind a tertiary tab.
- Avatar identity changes must use the approved cosmetic ownership system only.
- Settings owns persistent music volume, SFX volume and mute controls.
- Sign out requires explicit confirmation.
- Audio is cosmetic/experiential only and must never affect timing or competitive state.
- Device-visible quality is a release gate even when CI is green.
